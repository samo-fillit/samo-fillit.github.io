Privacy policy

Omarchy Calendar is a personal desktop widget used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

With the owner's permission it reads their Google Calendar events and creates or edits events they choose to add. Calendar data is stored only on the owner's own computer, is used only to show their calendar on their desktop, and is never shared with, sold to or sent to anyone else.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.
